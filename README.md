# Road Accident Data Analysis (2021-2022)

A comprehensive data analysis project examining road traffic accidents, casualties, weather conditions, and vehicle involvement trends using Python and Pandas.

## Project Overview
This repository contains an in-depth exploratory data analysis of road accident records covering the years 2021 and 2022. The goal is to uncover key patterns in accident severity, environmental factors, temporal distributions, and vehicle types to provide data-driven safety recommendations.

## Dataset Summary
- **Total Accidents Analyzed:** 307,973
- **Total Casualties:** 417,883
- **Total Vehicles Involved:** 563,302
- **Timeframe:** 2021 - 2022

## Key Insights & Findings
- **Accident Severity:** The vast majority of accidents (85.49%) are classified as slight, while serious accidents account for 13.23% and fatal accidents represent 1.28%.
- **Vehicle Involvement:** Private cars lead the statistics significantly (239,794 vehicles), followed by light commercial vans and high-capacity motorcycles.
- **Environmental Factors:** Over 79% of accidents occurred during clear weather (fine conditions without high winds) on dry road surfaces, indicating that high traffic density plays a more major role than adverse weather alone.
- **Geographical Distribution:** Urban areas experience a higher proportion of accidents (64.46%) compared to rural areas (35.54%).
- **Temporal Trends:** Fridays recorded the highest frequency of incidents (50,529), and November peaked among the months.
- **Speed Limits:** The 30 mph speed limit zones account for the highest concentration of accidents (200,040 incidents).

## Recommendations
1. **Urban Traffic Control:** Intensify monitoring and safety measures in urban centers where private car accidents concentrate.
2. **Single Carriageway Safety:** Implement enhanced road engineering and clear signage on single carriageway routes.
